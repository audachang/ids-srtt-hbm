The replacement retains the original time origin, integer-second onset convention, labels, amplitudes and one-second trial-slot convention. Each cycle is represented by the union of its logged one-second trial slots. Consecutive slots remain a single event; a documented gap splits that event into two events of the same condition. No event is extended across a gap. All runs retain 320 modeled trial-slot seconds, and all 56,320 logged trial onsets map to their correct condition. These are task-slot durations, not measured visual cue exposure durations; the RT/cue-duration question remains separate.
